# Home Screen Design Notes

- Header: Title 2 lines + subtitle; profile/avatar access in AppBar actions.
- Hero: Circle avatar with school icon; soft blue background.
- Sections:
  1) ProgressDashboard: overall progress bar + streak
  2) AiFeedbackPanel: sample feedback, one CTA
  3) AdaptiveRecommendations: 3 items with tags
  4) Continue Learning: list of LessonCard with badges and progress

- Bottom Navigation: Home, Lessons, Quizzes, Chat, Profile.
- Spacing: 16 outer padding; 8-16 inter-section spacing.
